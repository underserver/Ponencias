# Ponencias Application Usage Examples

## Submitting a New Paper
To submit a new paper, follow these steps:
1. Log in to the system using your credentials.
2. Navigate to the 'Mis Ponencias' section from the main menu.
3. Click on 'Nueva ponencia'.
4. Fill in the required fields such as title, date, summary, and thematic area. Ensure all fields are completed accurately.
5. Upload the necessary files, including the version with and without author names.
6. Click 'Save Changes' to submit your paper.

## Managing Evaluations
Evaluators can manage evaluations by following these steps:
1. Log in to the system using your credentials.
2. Navigate to the 'Evaluar Ponencias' section.
3. Select a paper from the list that you wish to evaluate.
4. Fill out the evaluation form by assigning a rating and providing observations.
5. Upload the dictamen document if required.
6. Submit the evaluation to complete the process.

## Viewing All Ponencias
1. Log in to your account.
2. Click on 'Ver otras ponencias' from the navigation menu.
3. Browse through the list of available ponencias with details like author and status.
4. Click on the title to view more details about a particular ponencia.
